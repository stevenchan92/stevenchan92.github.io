About me

Steven Chan is a New York-based photographer specializing in outdoor, travel, and lifestyle — drawn to light, texture, and the details that make a place feel lived-in.

His work has been featured in Fortune, Wired, TechCrunch, Washington Post, Nature, Passion Passport, and 16 Hours Magazine, and recognized by the International iPhone Photography Awards.

He has created content for brands including Y Combinator, Airbnb, Guesthop, Mobvoi, and more.

For brand partnerships, licensing, or commissions — reach out at s@stevenchan.co.
